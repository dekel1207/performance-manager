When critiquing performance review writing, do not call vague behaviors "attributes vs. events." That framing is imprecise and wrong.

**Why:** "Collaborated closely," "total ownership," "effective collaboration" are behavior descriptions, not attributes. Calling them attributes is inaccurate and confuses the writer. The actual problem is that they lack a concrete event to back them up.

**How to apply:** When a phrase is too vague, say "this needs a specific event to back it up" or "what exactly did they do here?" — not "this is an attribute, not an event." Reserve the attribute/event distinction for cases where someone is genuinely describing a personality trait (e.g. "is a strong technical leader", "is highly dedicated").
