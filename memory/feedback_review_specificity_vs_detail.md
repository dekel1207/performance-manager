description: Performance reviews should be specific about achievements but not exhaustively detailed — avoid listing small sub-tasks or micro-actions that make up a larger accomplishment

---

Performance reviews must be specific, but specific about the achievement — not about every small action that composed it.

**Why:** Listing granular details (e.g. which spike number a design doc came from, that someone answered 4 security questions, that they kept a Confluence page current) sounds disingenuous and buries the real achievement in noise. It reads like a task list, not a review of notable impact.

**How to apply:** When writing or improving a review paragraph, ask "is this detail describing the achievement or just describing the work that went into it?" If it's the latter, cut it. Name the outcome, not the steps. "Led the project from spike through beta launch" is enough — no need to call out the spike ticket, the specific security questions answered, or the Confluence page updates. Reserve specific details only for moments that are themselves the achievement (e.g. catching a critical bug, resolving a P0 same-day).
